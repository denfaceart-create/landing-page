## Alt Text Writing Framework for Face Painting

### Formula for Face Painting Alt Text:
**[Main Design/Character] + [Location on Face] + [Notable Colors] + [Special Details]**

### Key Principles:
1. **Be specific** - "Butterfly face paint" not just "face paint"
2. **Describe what you see** - Colors, design elements, placement
3. **Keep it concise** - Aim for 10-15 words max
4. **Don't say "image of" or "picture of"** - Screen readers already announce it's an image
5. **Mention the subject** - "Child with..." or "Young girl with..."
6. **Include notable features** - Glitter, sparkles, special effects

## Step-by-Step Process to Create Your Alt Text

### Step 1: Open Each Image
Go through your portfolio folder and open each image one by one.

### Step 2: Identify the Design
Ask yourself:
- What is the main design? (butterfly, superhero, animal, etc.)
- Where on the face is it painted? (full face, cheek, forehead)
- What are the dominant colors?
- Are there special effects? (glitter, sparkles, gems)

### Step 3: Write Alt Text Using This Template

```
[Main subject] + [Primary design] + [Key colors] + [Notable details]
```

**Examples:**
- "Child with blue butterfly face paint and silver glitter on both cheeks"
- "Spider-Man full face mask in red and black with web details"
- "Pink and purple unicorn design with rainbow mane and gold horn"
- "Orange tiger stripes with white muzzle and black nose"
- "Princess crown in pink with heart and star accents on forehead"

### Step 4: Keep It Concise
- Aim for 8-15 words
- Skip unnecessary words like "image of," "picture of," "showing"
- Focus on what makes this design unique and recognizable

### Step 5: Test It
Ask yourself: "If someone couldn't see this image, would they understand what's painted based on my description?"

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing each alt text, check:
- [ ] Does it describe the main design/character?
- [ ] Are the primary colors mentioned?
- [ ] Is it specific enough to distinguish from other images?
- [ ] Is it concise (8-15 words)?
- [ ] Does it avoid redundant phrases like "image of"?
- [ ] Would someone understand what's painted without seeing it?
- [ ] Is it written in natural language?

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Too Generic:** "Face painting artwork"
✅ **Specific:** "Rainbow butterfly design with blue wings"

❌ **Too Long:** "This is a beautiful image showing a young child who has been painted with a very detailed and colorful butterfly design featuring blue and purple colors and lots of sparkly glitter"
✅ **Concise:** "Blue and purple butterfly face paint with silver glitter"

❌ **Redundant:** "Image of face painting showing tiger design"
✅ **Direct:** "Orange tiger face paint with black stripes"

❌ **Missing Details:** "Animal face paint"
✅ **Descriptive:** "Orange tiger with black stripes and white muzzle"
